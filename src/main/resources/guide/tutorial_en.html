First-Order Differential Models Tutorial Guide

Direction Field Analyzer (dfieldX) — Version 1.1

This guide gathers the complete canonical catalog of first-order ordinary differential equations in the plane $(t, x)$ implemented in dfieldX. Each model provides analytical formulation, physical/biological interpretation, numerical exploration guidelines on the direction field, and classic conceptual references.

I. Population Dynamics & Biomathematics

1. Malthusian Model (Exponential Growth)

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Describes unrestricted per capita reproduction without spatial or food limitations:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = rx$$
  • Parameters: $r = 0.5$ (intrinsic Malthusian growth rate).
  • Equilibrium: $x = 0$ (repulsor if $r > 0$).
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Notice slopes depend strictly on $x$ (autonomous equation) and increase linearly with vertical height.
  2. Launch solutions with $x_0 > 0$ to verify unrestricted exponential growth $x(t) = x_0 e^{rt}$.

2. Verhulst Logistic Model

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Incorporates environmental carrying capacity $K$ with density-dependent self-limitation:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = rx\left(1 - \frac{x}{K}\right)$$
  • Parameters: $r = 0.8$, $K = 5.0$.
  • Equilibria: $x = 0$ (unstable/repulsor), $x = K$ (asymptotically stable attractor).
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Launch initial conditions above and below $K$. Notice solutions smoothly converge toward $x = K$.
  2. Observe the inflection point on the phase plot when $x = K/2$, where growth rate peaks.

3. Logistic Model with Constant Harvesting

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Models logistic growth subjected to constant extraction quota $h$:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = rx\left(1 - \frac{x}{K}\right) - h$$
  • Critical Threshold: Saddle-node bifurcation occurs at $h_c = \frac{rK}{4}$.
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Set $h < h_c$: two equilibria exist (stable node and unstable threshold).
  2. Increase $h > h_c$ using Parameter Sliders to witness extinction collapse across all initial states.

4. Logistic Model with Proportional Effort Harvesting

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Harvesting rate proportional to current population density ($E x$):

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = rx\left(1 - \frac{x}{K}\right) - Ex$$
  • Effective Growth: $\frac{dx}{dt} = (r - E)x\left(1 - \frac{x}{K(1 - E/r)}\right)$.
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Compare the stability transition when effort $E$ exceeds intrinsic rate $r$ (transcritical bifurcation).

5. Logistic Model with Periodic Seasonal Harvesting

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Non-autonomous ODE representing cyclical open/closed fishing or hunting seasons:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = rx\left(1 - \frac{x}{K}\right) - h_0\left(1 + A\sin(\omega t)\right)$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Notice direction field slopes now vary along time $t$, illustrating a non-autonomous regime.
  2. Inspect entrainment and periodic limit solutions around carrying capacity.

6. Population Model with Critical Allee Effect

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Features an undercrowding survival threshold $a$ below which populations go extinct:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = rx\left(1 - \frac{x}{K}\right)\left(\frac{x}{a} - 1\right)$$
  • Equilibria: $x = 0$ (stable extinction), $x = a$ (unstable threshold), $x = K$ (stable capacity).
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Release trajectories starting slightly above and slightly below $x = a$ to demonstrate bi-stability.

7. Gompertz Tumor Growth Dynamics

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Classic model in oncology where relative growth decay follows an exponential logarithmic law:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = -a x \ln(b x)$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Examine asymptotic saturation at tumor carrying capacity $x^* = 1/b$.

8. Von Bertalanffy Allometric Growth Equation

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Models organism body mass evolution balancing anabolism (surface area) and catabolism (volume):

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = a x^{2/3} - b x$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Trace trajectories approaching the stable adult biological size $x^* = (a/b)^3$.

II. Physics, Thermodynamics & Mechanics

9. Newton's Law of Cooling (Isothermal Medium)

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Heat exchange rate directly proportional to temperature discrepancy with ambient $T_{amb}$:

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - T_{amb})$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Observe the stable horizontal asymptote at $T = T_{amb}$.

10. Cooling with Oscillating Ambient Temperature

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Accounts for day/night circadian temperature variations:

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - (T_0 + A\sin(\omega t)))$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Notice thermal lag (phase shift) and attenuation of amplitude in interior temperature response.

11. Free Fall with Stokes Linear Drag

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Governs laminar low-speed fluid damping on falling bodies:

$$\frac{dv}{dt} = g - \frac{k}{m}v$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Observe global monotonic convergence toward terminal velocity $v_{term} = mg/k$.

12. Free Fall with Quadratic Aerodynamic Drag

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Turbulent Newtonian drag regime for macroscopic projectiles:

$$\frac{dv}{dt} = g - \gamma v^2$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Verify asymptotic approach to terminal velocity $v_{term} = \sqrt{g/\gamma}$.

13. Torricellian Tank Drainage

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Efflux speed from sharp-edged orifice under gravity:

$$\frac{dh}{dt} = -c\sqrt{h}$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Observe non-Lipschitz behavior at $h = 0$, leading to finite-time complete tank drainage.

14. RL / RC Electric Circuit with Sinusoidal Excitation

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Linear time-varying response of reactive electronic networks:

$$\frac{dI}{dt} + \frac{R}{L}I = \frac{V_0}{L}\cos(\omega t)$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Notice how transient state decays exponentially, settling into sinusoidal forced response.

15. Bateman Radioactive Decay Chain

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Intermediate nuclide daughter kinetics with concurrent generation and disintegration:

$$\frac{dx_2}{dt} = \lambda_1 N_0 e^{-\lambda_1 t} - \lambda_2 x_2$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Identify the peak secular/transient equilibrium moment of maximum daughter radioactivity.

III. Chemistry & Reaction Kinetics

16. First-Order Reversible Chemical Reaction

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Dynamic forward/backward equilibrium between chemical species $A \rightleftharpoons B$:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = k_f (A_0 - x) - k_r x$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Verify convergence toward chemical equilibrium concentration $x^* = \frac{k_f A_0}{k_f + k_r}$.

17. Isothermal Autocatalytic Reaction

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Kinetics where product molecule catalyzes its own synthesis:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = k x (C_0 - x)$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Notice characteristic sigmoidal progression from initial seed to total reagent transformation.

18. Continuous Stirred Tank Dilution (CSTR)

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Solute conservation in stirred volume with inflow and outflow streams:

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = \frac{q}{V}(C_{in} - C)$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Observe exponential wash-out or enrichment approaching steady state $C = C_{in}$.

IV. Geometry, Singularities & Bifurcations

19. Linear Equation with Oblique Isoclines and Asymptotes

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Canonical non-autonomous benchmark equation:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = t - x$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Activate Isoclines tool ($c = 0$): observe line $x = t$ dividing positive/negative slopes.
  2. Identify invariant slant asymptote $x(t) = t - 1$.

20. Riccati Equation with Regime Transition

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Nonlinear quadratic differential equation with variable coefficients:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = x^2 - t$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Examine the parabolic zero-isocline $t = x^2$ and boundary separating bounded and blow-up solutions.

21. Normal Form of Saddle-Node Bifurcation

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Fundamental topological mechanism creating/destroying pairs of equilibria:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = r + x^2$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Slide parameter $r$: for $r < 0$ there are two equilibria; for $r = 0$ they merge; for $r > 0$ flow is unobstructed.

22. Finite-Time Explosion Equation (Blow-up)

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PARAMETERS

Super-linear growth demonstrating breakdown of global existence theorem:

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = 1 + x^2$$
PRACTICAL EXPLORATION GUIDELINES
  1. Verify analytical solution $x(t) = \tan(t + C)$, experiencing vertical asymptotes at $t \to \pi/2$.
